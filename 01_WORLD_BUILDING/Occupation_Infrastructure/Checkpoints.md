## Overview
World-building reference sheet documenting the urban occupation systems established by Black Veil during the military lockdown and control of Santa Monica.

The infrastructure is designed to:
- maintain civilian obedience
- enforce curfew restrictions
- monitor resistance activity
- psychologically dominate the population
- centralize military authority

## Infrastructure Components

### Checkpoint Systems
Military-controlled access points featuring:
- retractable barricades
- armored vehicle screening
- civilian scanning zones
- fortified tactical positions
- drone surveillance integration

### Surveillance Towers
Advanced monitoring stations equipped with:
- thermal cameras
- sniper overwatch positions
- communications arrays
- drone landing pads
- infrared tracking systems

### Rooftop Control Posts
Temporary elevated tactical positions used for:
- sniper operations
- drone coordination
- night surveillance
- resistance monitoring
- rapid response deployment

### Refugee Processing Zones
Civilian containment and processing areas featuring:
- temporary holding tents
- floodlight security systems
- biometric processing
- armed perimeter control
- population management checkpoints

### Propaganda Infrastructure
Psychological messaging systems deployed throughout occupied zones:
- digital billboards
- loudspeaker systems
- obedience messaging
- anti-resistance broadcasts
- civilian intimidation campaigns
