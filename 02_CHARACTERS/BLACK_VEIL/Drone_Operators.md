## Overview
Integrated aerial surveillance and tactical drone ecosystem deployed by the Black Veil faction during the occupation of Santa Monica.

The drone network serves multiple operational roles:
- civilian surveillance
- psychological intimidation
- reconnaissance
- tactical combat support
- crowd suppression
- curfew enforcement

## Drone Classes

### 1. Recon Drone
Small backpack-sized quadrotor unit used for:
- rooftop surveillance
- civilian tracking
- thermal scanning
- night patrols
- resistance detection

### 2. Attack Drone
Weaponized tactical support drone equipped with:
- tactical spotlight
- suppression weapon system
- smoke deployment
- micro rocket capability
- laser targeting

### 3. Heavy Scan Drone
Large hovering surveillance platform featuring:
- advanced thermal optics
- aerial command uplink
- crowd tracking systems
- searchlight systems
- command coordination

## Visual Characteristics
- Matte black military finish
- Infrared red sensor lighting
- Silent rotor systems
- Tactical industrial design
- Psychological intimidation aesthetic
- Near-future grounded realism

## Psychological Warfare Function
The drone presence is intentionally designed to:
- create constant civilian anxiety
- eliminate feelings of privacy
- reinforce occupation authority
- disrupt resistance organization
- maintain perpetual surveillance pressure
