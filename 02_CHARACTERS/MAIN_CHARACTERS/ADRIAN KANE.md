
### Role:

Leader of Black Veil

### Age:

Mid-to-late 40s

### Personality:

- Hyper-intelligent
- Emotionally detached
- Calm and surgical
- Speaks quietly instead of aggressively
- Philosophically authoritarian
- Believes order justifies brutality
- Never loses composure
- Manipulative but believable

### Psychological Traits:

- Sees humanity as self-destructive
- Believes fear creates stability
- Does not view himself as evil
- Treats warfare like psychological engineering
- Obsessed with control systems

### Strengths:

- Strategic genius
- Psychological warfare mastery
- Long-term planning
- Charismatic authority
- Ruthless efficiency

### Weaknesses:

- Lack of empathy
- Underestimates emotional resistance
- Obsession with control
- Cannot understand sacrifice rooted in love

### Visual Energy:

Stillness, cold authority, minimalist movements, controlled voice, silhouette framing.

### Core Theme:

> Order through domination.
