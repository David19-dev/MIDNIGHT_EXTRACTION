# Avah_Reyes
![[Create_a_professional_character_reference_202605230822.jpeg]]

### Role:

Emergency nurse at Saint Mercy Hospital

### Age:

Early 30s

### Personality:

- Compassionate under pressure
- Emotionally resilient
- Protective instinct toward civilians
- Calm voice during chaos
- Naturally self-sacrificing
- Strong moral compass
- Quietly stubborn
- Carries emotional exhaustion behind professionalism

### Psychological Traits:

- Suppresses fear to protect others
- Feels responsible for everyone around her
- Hates abandoning people
- Trauma-sensitive but highly functional
- Becomes stronger the worse situations get

### Strengths:

- Leadership during crisis
- Human empathy
- Medical improvisation
- Emotional intelligence
- Trustworthy presence

### Weaknesses:

- Overworks herself
- Refuses to emotionally detach
- Survivor guilt tendencies
- Sometimes hesitates to save herself

### Visual Energy:

Soft but determined eyes, tired posture, hospital scrubs stained during later sequences, emotional realism.

### Core Theme:

> Humanity surviving inside collapse.
