
# KILEY MORGAN

### Role:

Mother inside Saint Mercy maternity ward

### Age: 28

### Personality:

- Warm
- Gentle
- Emotionally vulnerable
- Strong maternal instinct
- Quietly brave
- Not physically strong but emotionally enduring

### Psychological Traits:

- Terrified of losing her children
- Panic gradually transforms into survival instinct
- Finds strength through motherhood
- Emotionally human representation of civilian suffering

### Strengths:

- Emotional endurance
- Protective instinct
- Human vulnerability
- Adaptability under fear

### Weaknesses:

- No combat experience
- Easily overwhelmed initially
- Anxiety under pressure

### Visual Energy:

Soft emotional cinematography, intimate close-ups, trembling hands, exhausted eyes.

### Core Theme:

> Innocence trapped in war.
