## CHARACTER OVERVIEW

**Full Name:** SPC Taha  
**Role:** Main protagonist / military operator  
**Affiliation:** Friendly Forces  
**Rank:** Specialist (SPC)  
**Height:** 6'5  
**Age:** 29  

---

## PERSONALITY

SPC Taha is calm under pressure, observant, disciplined, and emotionally grounded.  
Unlike louder military personalities, he processes situations internally and reacts with precision instead of panic.

He carries:
- strong survival instincts
- tactical intelligence
- emotional restraint
- quiet leadership energy
- deep loyalty toward his team

Even during collapse, he remains focused on protecting civilians and completing the mission.

---

## PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE

### Core Traits
- Tactical
- Loyal
- Resilient
- Emotionally controlled
- Protective

### Internal Conflict
Taha struggles between:
- following orders
- and protecting innocent civilians trapped inside occupied Santa Monica.

### Emotional Baseline
Quiet intensity mixed with accumulated fatigue from prolonged combat operations.
