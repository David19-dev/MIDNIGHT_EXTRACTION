# Shot 16 — Families Fleeing City Collapse

![[SHOT_16_Families_Fleeing_City_Collapse.jpg.jpeg]]
## Sequence
Prologue → Sequence 3 → The City Falls

## Description
Terrified civilians attempt to flee downtown Santa Monica as Black Veil attacks intensify. Families are separated amid emergency barricades, fires, police sirens, and aerial drone surveillance.

## Scene Purpose
Shows the human collapse occurring during the active fall of the city before full occupation control is established.

## Visual Notes
- Panicked civilians
- Mothers carrying children
- Rain-soaked streets
- Emergency response breakdown
- Smoke and fire in background
- Red drone surveillance beams
- Curfew messaging beginning to appear

## Atmosphere
Panic  
Civil collapse  
Mass confusion  
Fear and displacement

## Continuity
Occurs during the transitional phase between the invasion assault and the establishment of organized occupation control.
