# Shot 17 — Bridge Escape Failure

![[SHOT_17_Bridge_Escape_Failure.jpg.jpeg]]
## Sequence
Prologue → Sequence 3 → The City Falls

## Description
Thousands of civilians attempting to flee Santa Monica are stopped at a heavily militarized bridge checkpoint as Black Veil forces establish emergency containment barriers during the collapse of the city.

## Scene Purpose
Shows the moment civilians realize escape is no longer possible and the city is becoming trapped under hostile control.

## Visual Notes
- Massive civilian traffic jam
- Armored Black Veil vehicles
- Drone surveillance with red scan beams
- Rain-covered bridge environment
- Curfew warning signs
- Soldiers aiming rifles at crowd
- Smoke and fire near abandoned vehicles

## Atmosphere
Panic  
Entrapment  
Hopeless evacuation  
Escalating authoritarian control

## Continuity
Occurs during the final stage of Santa Monica’s collapse before Black Veil fully establishes occupation systems.
