# Shot 23 — Black Veil Propaganda Takeover

![[SHOT_23_Black_Veil_Propaganda_Takeover.jpg]]

## Sequence
Prologue → Sequence 4 → The Occupation

## Scene Purpose
Black Veil establishes psychological dominance over Santa Monica through massive propaganda displays, aerial drone patrols, and public intimidation.

## Description
A rain-soaked downtown intersection is overtaken by giant Black Veil propaganda billboards displaying the slogan:

> “ORDER THROUGH CONTROL”

Heavy surveillance drones hover above civilians while armored occupation vehicles secure the streets below. Citizens watch in fear as the city transitions from invasion to full occupation.

## Visual Notes
- Giant illuminated Black Veil propaganda screens
- Red surveillance drone lights
- Rain reflections on asphalt
- Burning debris and smoke haze
- Civilians documenting the occupation with phones
- Cold blue-gray palette with aggressive red highlights
