
# Scene 1 — “The Call Before Midnight”

## Sequence 1 — Arrival at Fort Leonard Wood

# Shot 10
![[s10.jpeg]]
### Shot Type

Wide Squad Assembly Shot

### Location

Deployment Staging Area — Fort Leonard Wood

---

## Visual Description

The squad gathers together for the first time inside the deployment staging area beneath harsh fluorescent military lighting.

Rows of tactical gear cases, ammunition crates, radio packs, and weapons line the concrete floor around them while soldiers and mechanics continue rushing through the background preparing for emergency deployment.

The core team stands together near a large deployment board displaying convoy routes and emergency mobilization data:

- SPC Taha carrying his deployment bag over one shoulder
    
- PFC Sara standing beside him adjusting tactical gloves
    
- PFC Jerome quietly checking his phone one last time
    
- SSG Jeffcoat securing rifle magazines into his vest
    
- LT Lemay standing in front of the group holding a classified tactical folder
    

For the first time:  
the audience sees the full team together before entering Santa Monica.

Mounted televisions nearby continue broadcasting fragmented emergency footage:

- burning intersections
    
- military response failures
    
- Black Veil checkpoints
    
- emergency broadcasts cutting to static
    

The room grows quieter emotionally around the squad.

Everyone understands:  
this deployment is different.

---
