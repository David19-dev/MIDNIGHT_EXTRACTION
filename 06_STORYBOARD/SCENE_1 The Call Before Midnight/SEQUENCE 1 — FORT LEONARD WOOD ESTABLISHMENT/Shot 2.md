# Scene 1 — “The Call Before Midnight”
## Sequence 1 — Arrival at Fort Leonard Wood
### Shot 2
![[s2.jpeg]]
![[s22.jpeg]]
**Shot Type:**  
Medium Wide Tracking Shot

**Location:**  
Motor Pool — Fort Leonard Wood

---

## Visual Description

Rows of LMTVs and Humvees sit under heavy rain while mechanics and infantry soldiers rapidly prepare vehicles for emergency deployment. Headlights glow through fog. Soldiers load ammunition crates, fuel cans, and tactical gear into trucks.

A convoy begins forming in the background.

SPC Taha walks through the motor pool carrying his helmet and rifle bag, observing the unusual level of activity around him. Soldiers avoid eye contact. Everyone feels the urgency.

---

## Main Focus

Introduce Taha into the military environment while building tension.

---

## Characters Present

- SPC Taha
- Mechanics
- Infantry soldiers
- Vehicle crews

---

## Camera Style

Side tracking shot following Taha walking between military vehicles.

---

## Lighting

- White floodlights reflecting on rain-soaked metal
- Orange maintenance lights
- Flashing red warning lights in background
- Fog diffusing headlights

---

## Sound Design

- Metal equipment clanking
- Engines starting

- Helicopter rotors overhead

## Mood

Urgent military preparation.  
The mission already feels different from a normal deployment.
