
# Scene 1 — “The Call Before Midnight”
## Sequence 1 — Arrival at Fort Leonard Wood
### Shot 3
![[S3.jpeg]]
**Shot Type:**  
Medium Interior Character Shot

**Location:**  
Barracks Hallway — Fort Leonard Wood

---

## Visual Description

Inside a dim military barracks hallway, SPC Taha walks alone toward his room while soldiers rush past carrying duffel bags and combat gear. Fluorescent ceiling lights flicker slightly above wet boot prints on the polished floor.

Television screens mounted on the hallway walls display fragmented emergency news broadcasts from California. Audio cuts in and out through static.

A distant thunder rumble shakes the building slightly.

Taha slows down near one screen showing partial footage of Santa Monica burning before the signal abruptly cuts black.

He stares silently.

Something feels wrong.

---

## Main Focus

Show the first emotional connection between Taha and the unfolding crisis.

This is the moment where the mission becomes personal emotionally, even before deployment orders arrive.

---

## Characters Present

- SPC Taha
- Passing soldiers
- Background officers

---

## Camera Style

Slow forward dolly movement toward Taha as he stops near the television screen.

Subtle handheld vibration for realism.

---

## Lighting

- Cold fluorescent military hallway lights
- Soft blue emergency broadcast glow from television screens
- Dark shadows between hallway sections
- Occasional red alarm reflection from outside vehicles

---

## Color Grading

- Cold desaturated blue-gray palette
- Muted skin tones
- Soft cyan shadows
- Slight green fluorescent tint
- Deep blacks with subtle film grain
- Low saturation military realism
- Red emergency lights selectively emphasized
- Rain reflections slightly elevated for cinematic contrast

Reference mood:
*Zero Dark Thirty* meets *Sicario* military atmosphere.

---

## Sound Design

- Muffled radio chatter
- Combat boots echoing through hallway
- Distant thunder
- Static from emergency broadcasts
- Soldiers speaking quietly in background

---

## Mood

Uneasy anticipation.

The audience should feel that the world is beginning to fracture.

---

## Dialogue

**News Reporter (TV Static):**  
“—unconfirmed reports of armed personnel appearing throughout Santa Monica—”

**Broadcast cuts to static.**

**Passing Soldier:**  
“You saw the footage?”

**Another Soldier:**  
“They locked the entire coast down…”
