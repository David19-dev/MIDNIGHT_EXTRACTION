## Visual Description

A close-up shot of official deployment orders spread across a scratched metal preparation table under harsh fluorescent lighting. Rainwater drips from SPC Taha’s sleeve onto the documents while his deployment bag rests beside the table.

Stamped across the top page in bold military lettering:

> EMERGENCY MOBILIZATION

Beside the paperwork:
- loaded rifle magazines
- military gloves
- folded map of California
- radio headset
- dog tags
- flashlight
- partially opened ammo case

A red emergency light slowly pulses across the documents, creating an ominous rhythm.

Taha’s hand enters frame, gripping the edge of the orders tightly for a moment before releasing.

---

## Main Focus

Reveal the seriousness of the mission.

Transition from routine military preparation into emergency wartime deployment.
