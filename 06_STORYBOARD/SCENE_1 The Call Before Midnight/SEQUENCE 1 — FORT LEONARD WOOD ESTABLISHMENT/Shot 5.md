
# Scene 1 — “The Call Before Midnight”

## Sequence 1 — Arrival at Fort Leonard Wood

# Shot 5
![[s5.jpeg]]
### Shot Type

Medium Shot

### Location

Barracks Equipment Room — Fort Leonard Wood

---

## Visual Description

PFC Sara enters the crowded military equipment room carrying a heavy tactical deployment bag over one shoulder and an M4 rifle held low across her chest. Rainwater drips from her combat uniform after crossing the base outside.

The room is packed with soldiers rapidly preparing combat gear under harsh fluorescent lighting. Open weapon cases, ammunition crates, radio systems, helmets, and body armor cover long metal tables.

Mounted televisions hanging on the walls display fragmented footage from Santa Monica:

- burning intersections
    
- police sirens
    
- emergency broadcasts cutting to static
    

Sara walks through the chaos calmly.

Across the room, SPC Taha stands silently reviewing deployment orders beside his deployment bag.

For a brief moment:  
their eyes meet.

Neither speaks.

The emotional connection is subtle, restrained, and realistic.

---

## Main Focus

Introduce Sara’s emotional presence while establishing the quiet connection between her and Taha before deployment.

---

## Characters Present

- PFC Sara
    
- SPC Taha
    
- Infantry soldiers
    
- Armorers
    
- Communications personnel
    

---

## Camera Style

Slow cinematic dolly movement following Sara entering the room before settling into a soft over-the-shoulder composition toward Taha.

Background activity remains slightly blurred using shallow depth of field.

---

## Lighting

- Harsh fluorescent military lighting
    
- Soft red emergency light reflections
    
- Cold rain light entering through side windows
    
- Metallic reflections on tactical gear
    
- Dim shadow falloff in background corners
    

---

## Color Grading

### Primary Palette

- Cold military blue-gray
    
- Desaturated olive green
    
- Pale fluorescent white
    
- Tactical emergency red
    

### Shadow Treatment

- Slight teal shadows
    
- Deep matte blacks
    
- Wet reflective surfaces
    

### Emotional Tone

The grading should feel:

- emotionally restrained
    
- realistic
    
- cold
    
- tense beneath the surface
    

Subtle warmth appears only on skin tones during the eye contact moment between Taha and Sara.

---

## Sound Design

- Rain hitting exterior windows
    
- Soldiers loading magazines
    
- Velcro straps tightening
    
- Helicopter rotors outside
    
- Distant radio chatter
    
- Boots moving across concrete floors
    

---

## Mood

Quiet emotional tension before war.

The audience should feel:  
something important exists between them,  
even if neither says it aloud.

None.
