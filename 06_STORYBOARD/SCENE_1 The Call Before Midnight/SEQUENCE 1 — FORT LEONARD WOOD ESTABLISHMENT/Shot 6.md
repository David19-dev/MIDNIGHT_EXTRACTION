
# Scene 1 — “The Call Before Midnight”

## Sequence 1 — Arrival at Fort Leonard Wood

# Shot 6
![[s6.jpeg]]
### Shot Type

Two-Shot / Medium Close-Up

### Location

Equipment Room Exit Corridor — Fort Leonard Wood

---

## Visual Description

SPC Taha exits the crowded equipment room carrying his deployment bag over one shoulder while tightening the strap of his plate carrier with his free hand.

PFC Sara catches up beside him in the narrow military corridor outside the preparation area. Soldiers continue moving quickly around them carrying rifles, radios, ammunition crates, and combat gear toward deployment staging zones.

The environment remains tense and active, but emotionally the moment briefly slows down.

Sara walks beside Taha while studying him carefully.  
Taha remains focused straight ahead.

Red emergency lights pulse softly across the corridor walls while fluorescent ceiling lights flicker above them.

For the first time in the scene:  
the audience feels the quiet emotional connection between them.

---

## Main Focus

Establish subtle chemistry and emotional realism between Taha and Sara before deployment.

This moment must feel:

- restrained
    
- believable
    
- military grounded
    
- emotionally unfinished
    

---

## Characters Present

- SPC Taha
    
- PFC Sara
    
- Passing infantry soldiers
    

---

## Camera Style

Slow handheld tracking shot moving beside both characters as they walk through the corridor.

Very shallow depth of field isolates them emotionally from surrounding military activity.

Slight natural camera sway for realism.

---

## Lighting

- Flickering fluorescent hallway lights
    
- Soft red emergency reflections
    
- Cool industrial overhead lighting
    
- Dim shadows along corridor edges
    

---

## Color Grading

### Primary Palette

- Cold blue-gray military tones
    
- Desaturated green hues
    
- Soft tactical reds
    
- Muted fluorescent whites
    

### Contrast Style

- Deep matte blacks
    
- Slight teal shadow tones
    
- Soft highlight bloom from overhead lighting
    

### Emotional Tone

The grading should feel:

- emotionally restrained
    
- intimate without romance
    
- tense beneath the silence
    
- grounded and realistic
    

Slight warmth appears subtly on skin tones during moments of eye contact.

---

## Sound Design

- Boots echoing through hallway
    
- Distant helicopter rotors outside
    
- Radio chatter
    
- Gear straps moving against body armor
    
- Soft fluorescent electrical hum
    
- Soldiers rushing past in background
    

---

## Mood

Quiet connection before war.

The audience should feel:  
these two understand each other without needing many words.

---

## Dialogue

**Sara (slight smirk):**  
“You ever relax?”

**Taha:**  
“After the mission.”

Sara looks at him for another second.

Taha keeps walking forward.
