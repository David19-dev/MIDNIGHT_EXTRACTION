# Scene 1 — “The Call Before Midnight”

## Sequence 1 — Arrival at Fort Leonard Wood

# Shot 7 (Updated)

### Shot Type

Medium Character Introduction Shot

### Location

Fort Leonard Wood Preparation Area — Night

---

## Visual Description

Jerome enters the crowded deployment preparation area carrying a large tactical deployment bag over one shoulder while holding his combat helmet in his hand. Soldiers move rapidly around the room preparing weapons, radios, ammunition, and combat gear for emergency deployment.

Mounted televisions hanging from support columns display fragmented emergency footage from Santa Monica:

- burning streets
    
- flashing police lights
    
- emergency broadcasts cutting to static
    
- Black Veil checkpoint footage
    

Jerome repeatedly checks his phone while walking through the preparation area, waiting for a reply that never comes.

Unlike the focused military discipline surrounding him, Jerome appears emotionally distracted and anxious.

He briefly stops beside a locker row and stares at unread messages sent to Avah.

No response.

After a long breath, he slips the phone back into his pocket and continues toward the squad.

---

## Main Focus

Introduce Jerome’s emotional vulnerability and establish his connection to Avah before deployment.

The audience should feel:

- anxiety
    
- emotional attachment
    
- uncertainty
    

---

## Characters Present

- PFC Jerome
    
- Infantry soldiers
    
- Armorers
    
- Communications personnel
    

---

## Camera Style

Slow handheld medium tracking shot following Jerome through the preparation area.

Slight natural handheld instability reflects emotional stress.

Background military activity remains slightly blurred using shallow depth of field.

---

## Lighting

- Soft red emergency light reflections
    
- Metallic reflections from tactical equipment
    
- Dim industrial shadows around weapon racks

### Emotional Tone

The grading should feel:

- emotionally restrained
    
- anxious
    
- cold
    
- realistic
    

Jerome’s face should carry slightly warmer tones than the environment to preserve emotional humanity.

## Mood

Emotional anxiety before deployment.

Jerome already fears:  
someone he cares about may still be trapped inside Santa Monica.
