# Scene 1 — “The Call Before Midnight”

## Sequence 1 — Arrival at Fort Leonard Wood

# Shot 8

![[s8.jpeg]]

### Shot Type

Medium Close-Up Character Shot

### Location

Weapons Preparation Area — Fort Leonard Wood

---

## Visual Description

SSG Jeffcoat stands at a long metal weapons table inside the deployment preparation area methodically inspecting rifles, magazines, and ammunition under harsh fluorescent military lighting.

Unlike the younger soldiers rushing around the room, Jeffcoat moves with calm precision and veteran discipline.

Several disassembled rifles, ammunition boxes, tactical gloves, and radio equipment are spread across the table in front of him.

Mounted televisions nearby continue broadcasting fragmented emergency footage from Santa Monica:

- burning intersections
    
- civilians running
    
- Black Veil checkpoint footage
    
- emergency broadcasts failing
    

Jeffcoat briefly pauses while watching the footage.

His expression hardens.

Without saying anything, he resumes preparing weapons.

---

## Main Focus

Introduce Jeffcoat as:

- experienced
    
- disciplined
    
- emotionally controlled
    
- quietly affected by the situation
    

The audience should feel:  
this soldier has seen war before,  
but this situation feels different even to him.

---

## Characters Present

- SSG Jeffcoat
    
- Passing soldiers
    
- Armorers
    
- Communications personnel
    

---

## Camera Style

Slow cinematic push-in toward Jeffcoat while he prepares weapons.

Very shallow depth of field isolates him from the chaotic military activity surrounding him.

---

## Lighting

- Harsh fluorescent military lighting
    
- Cold reflections across weapon surfaces
    
- Soft red emergency light pulses
    
- Dark industrial shadows around equipment racks
    

---

## Color Grading

### Primary Palette

- Cold steel-blue military tones
    
- Desaturated olive drab greens
    
- Pale fluorescent whites
    
- Deep matte blacks
    

### Contrast Style

- Slight cyan shadow tones
    
- Metallic texture emphasis
    
- Controlled low-saturation realism
    

### Emotional Tone

The grading should feel:

- cold
    
- disciplined
    
- emotionally restrained
    
- militarily authentic
    

Jeffcoat’s face should carry slightly warmer midtones to preserve human realism beneath the cold environment.

## Mood

Quiet veteran focus before deployment.

Jeffcoat understands:  
this operation may become much worse than command is admitting.

---

## Dialogue

**Background TV Reporter:**  
“Authorities have lost multiple communication sectors across Santa Monica…”

**Passing Soldier:**  
“Black Veil’s everywhere now…”

Jeffcoat remains silent.
