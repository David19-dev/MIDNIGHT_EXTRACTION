# Scene 1 — “The Call Before Midnight”

## Sequence 1 — Arrival at Fort Leonard Wood

# Shot 9

![[s9.jpeg]]
### Shot Type

Low-Angle Leadership Introduction Shot

### Location

Deployment Preparation Floor — Fort Leonard Wood

---

## Visual Description

LT Lemay walks through the crowded deployment preparation floor with calm tactical focus while soldiers and mechanics continue preparing for emergency mobilization around him.

Unlike the rushed movement surrounding him, Lemay moves with complete control and discipline.

He carries a classified tactical folder under one arm while scanning the preparation area:

- weapons tables
    
- vehicle crews
    
- communications stations
    
- soldiers loading equipment
    

As he walks through the room, soldiers subtly straighten posture and clear pathways instinctively.

Mounted televisions continue showing fragmented emergency broadcasts from Santa Monica:

- fires burning through intersections
    
- evacuation chaos
    
- military checkpoints
    
- Black Veil drone footage
    

Lemay briefly stops near the squad preparation area and watches Taha, Sara, Jerome, and Jeffcoat silently preparing gear.

He already understands:  
this mission will be unlike anything they trained for.

---

## Main Focus

Establish LT Lemay as:

- disciplined
    
- composed
    
- respected
    
- tactically intelligent
    
- emotionally controlled under pressure
    

The audience should feel:  
he is carrying the weight of command alone.

## Camera Style

Slow low-angle tracking shot following Lemay walking through the preparation floor.

The camera slightly pushes forward as he surveys the room.

Background activity remains slightly blurred using shallow depth of field.

---
