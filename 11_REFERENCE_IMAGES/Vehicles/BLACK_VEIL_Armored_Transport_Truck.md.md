## Description
Primary armored personnel carrier used by Black Veil occupation forces throughout Santa Monica during the invasion and containment phases.

The vehicle is designed for:
- urban troop deployment
- intimidation patrols
- rapid checkpoint reinforcement
- civilian suppression operations

## Key Features
- Matte black ceramic composite armor
- Reinforced ballistic windows
- Roof-mounted remote weapon station
- Drone deployment hatch
- Interior tactical compartment
- Heavy all-terrain tires
- Rear hydraulic troop ramp
- Integrated surveillance communications array

## Visual Language
The truck reflects Black Veil doctrine:
- minimalistic authoritarian design
- aggressive silhouette
- industrial realism
- near-future tactical militarism

## Cinematic Usage
Appears during:
- occupation convoys
- bridge checkpoints
- curfew enforcement
- civilian containment operations
- rapid tactical insertions
